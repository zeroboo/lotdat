2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following categories:

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: If you create an account or sign in via Firebase Authentication, we may collect your email address, display name, and authentication credentials.
  • Lottery Ticket Data: Photos of lottery tickets you scan using the camera or import from your photo library. These images are processed locally on your device using ML Kit Text Recognition (OCR) to extract ticket numbers.
  • Support Communications: If you contact us for support, we may collect any information you voluntarily provide in those communications.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and general device settings (collected via Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics).
  • Usage Data: App interaction data such as screens viewed, features used, session duration, and app events (collected via Firebase Analytics).
  • Crash and Performance Data: Crash logs, stack traces, and performance diagnostics to help us identify and fix bugs (collected via Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Advertising Data: Advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID) and ad interaction data collected by Google AdMob to serve and measure advertisements.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Device tokens for delivering push notifications (via Expo Notifications).
  • In-App Purchase Data: Transaction records processed through Google Play or Apple App Store billing systems.

2.3 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Saved Tickets: Lottery ticket data (numbers, dates, results) is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data is not transmitted to our servers unless you explicitly sync your account.
  • App Preferences: Settings and preferences you configure within the App are stored locally.

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Firebase services employ industry-standard security measures including encryption at rest.

  • Authentication is handled through Firebase Auth with secure token-based mechanisms.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
